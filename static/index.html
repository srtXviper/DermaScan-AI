PHOTO GUIDE

Make every image count.

Good image quality can help make an image-classification experiment more consistent. It cannot guarantee a correct result.

01
☼

Use soft, even light

Choose a bright room or indirect daylight. Avoid harsh flash, deep shadows and colour filters.

02
◎

Keep the image in focus

Hold the camera steady and tap to focus if your phone supports it. Avoid blurry or pixelated photos.

03
▣

Frame the area clearly

Include the full area of interest with a little surrounding skin. Do not crop away relevant context.

04
◇

Keep it natural

Use an unedited image. Do not apply beauty filters, colour adjustments or sharpening effects.

!
Seek professional care when concerned.

Do not delay medical advice because of an AI result. A clinician can examine your skin and consider your history.

MODEL TRANSPARENCY

Know what is under the hood.

DermaScan AI is an experimental academic prototype built to demonstrate an image-classification workflow.

CURRENT MODEL

ResNet18 CNN

A convolutional neural network architecture that learns visual patterns from labelled training images and produces scores across the labels it was trained to recognise.

Limitations and responsible use

The training dataset may not represent all populations, skin tones, cameras or real-world conditions. Scores are not calibrated medical probabilities. The model has not been clinically validated and must not be used for diagnosis, triage, or treatment decisions.
